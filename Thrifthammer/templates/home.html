The Smarter Way to Buy Warhammer 40,000

Why Warhammer Prices Vary So Much

Games Workshop sells Warhammer 40,000 kits at full MSRP, but authorised retailers, independent stores, and marketplace sellers regularly offer the same kits for 15% to 25% less. That gap exists because different retailers have different margins, buy in bulk, and compete for your business. The problem is that tracking all of them manually is a full-time job.

ThriftHammer automatically tracks prices across the major US Warhammer retailers every day, so you always know where to buy for the best price without spending hours checking each site individually.

How Much Can You Actually Save?

The savings add up fast when you're building a full army. A single Space Marine Intercessor Squad retails at $55 MSRP but regularly sells for $40–$47 at third-party retailers. That's up to $15 saved on one kit. A typical 2,000-point army requires 10–15 kits. At average ThriftHammer discounts, that's $150–$250 saved on a single army.
